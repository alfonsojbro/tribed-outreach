## 1. Fingerprint rules

<!-- Filled from the voice interview + promoted corrections. Until populated, the core principles in SKILL.md are the fallback. -->

**Every message closes on an ask, and the ask is sized to the stage.** (Promoted 2026-08-26 from the 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-26 corrections.)

- First touch, the link does not exist yet: ask to send it. "i made an initial version of `<AppName>`, can i send it over?" Past tense, named for their audience, one small yes.
- The message that DELIVERS the link: state what it is, with conviction. Name it in THEIR structure (their modules, their weeks, their method), then close on one line of belief in their system. "here it is. `<method>` as an app, run daily under your name. `<their structure, in their words>`. `<tracked link>` a system like this deserves to be lived, not just read." (Superseded 2026-09-25, see the correction below: the old version down-ranked the artifact.)
- **Never shrink the work in a delivery or follow-up.** Banned: "just", "only", "rough", "quick", "little", "initial version", "first cut", "early version", "nothing fancy", and anything that invites criticism: "tell me what's wrong with it", "be honest", "roast it", "what would you change", "no pressure". These read as insecurity and they hand the prospect the job of judging us. We believe in what we built: say so plainly. The follow-up ladder carries the questions; the delivery carries the conviction.
- "initial version" stays allowed in the FIRST touch only, where the link does not exist yet ("i made an initial version of `<AppName>`, can i send it over?"), because there it says the product is already underway.
- Never close on the build story, on why you made it, or on a reaction-to-the-product question.

- 2026-08-20: The connection note closes on the ASK, not on the build story. He cut the whole "why I built it" clause. Mine: "I built your course as an app to see whether therapists would actually use it between cases. It already exists." -> His: "I made an initial version of the Running Repairs app, want me to send it over?"
- 2026-08-20: Name the app after THEIR method, never "a demo app". Generic: "I made a demo app around your method." His: "the Running Repairs app", "the Only You app", "the Airway Mouth Doctor app", "the Mini Residency app". The possessive does the personalization work that an extra sentence used to.
- 2026-08-20: Say "initial version", not "demo". A demo is a sales prop; an initial version is their product already underway. Rotate the phrasing across a batch: "I made an initial version of..." / "I built a first version of..." / "I put together an early version of..." / "There's a first version of ... sitting ready" / "I made a first cut of...".
- 2026-08-20: Batch-level tell, caught by Alfonso reading five notes side by side ("is that the best you can think of"). Four of five ended in the identical sentence "It already exists." Rotating the OPENER is not enough; the CLOSING construction must vary across the batch too, because prospects in the same niche know each other.
- 2026-08-26 (SUPERSEDED 2026-09-25, do not follow the down-rank move): The message that DELIVERS the link closes on a low-commitment yes about THEIR clients, not on a curiosity question about the product. Mine: "Here it is <link>, curious what you make of the nervous system side?" -> His: "It's just an initial version, I laid it out as your twelve weeks so each one carries between the sessions. Do you think this is something the women in Fabulous 9 could use?" Two moves in one line: down-rank the artifact ("just an initial version") so there is nothing to judge yet, then ask a question they can say yes to without committing to anything. Personalize the middle clause with THEIR own structure (her twelve weeks), so the ask is about their programme rather than our build.
- 2026-08-26: Given two closes, he took the one that does not put a claim in the prospect's mouth. Rejected: "I built it around the between-sessions gap, which is where you said founders and CEOs usually lose it. Do you think this is something your women could use?" Chosen: the plain version with no assertion about her clients. Do not make someone agree with your read of their own business in order to answer your question.
- 2026-08-29: The delivery-message ask is "could use", never a behavior forecast, and never with doubt planted in it. Mine: "do you think the men in KING MAKER would actually open something like this?" -> Rule applied: "do you think this is something the men in KING MAKER could use?" Two faults in mine: "would actually open" imports the failure mode (apps nobody opens) into the question and asks her to defend against it, and "would ... open" asks her to PREDICT member behavior, which is a harder yes than "could use", a possibility. The ask names their people ("the men in KING MAKER"), not "something like this".
- 2026-09-25: The delivery message states the work with conviction, never down-ranks it and never invites criticism. This SUPERSEDES the 2026-08-26 "down-rank the artifact" rule and its "It's just an initial version" example. Mine: "here it is. built it around the modules 00 to 09, with the exercises kept in your intent action payoff format. the openings and walls chart is in there too. <link> it's an initial version, tell me what's wrong with it." -> His: "here it is. ku/no as an app, run daily under your name. 00 to 09 in order, the exercises exactly as you wrote them, openings and walls built in. <link> a system like this deserves to be lived, not just read." His reason: "tell me whats wrong with it sounds insecure ... this is the next generation of education! we must believe in it with our souls." Two moves: name their structure as a finished fact ("00 to 09 in order", "exactly as you wrote them"), then end on belief in their system, not on a request for their verdict.
