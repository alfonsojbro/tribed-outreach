## 1. Fingerprint rules

<!-- Filled from the voice interview + promoted corrections. Until populated, the core principles in SKILL.md are the fallback. -->

**Every message closes on an ask, and the ask is sized to the stage.** (Promoted 2026-08-26 from the 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-26 corrections.)

- First touch, the link does not exist yet: ask to send it. "i made an initial version of `<AppName>`, can i send it over?" Past tense, named for their audience, one small yes.
- The message that DELIVERS the link: down-rank the artifact, then ask a low-commitment yes about THEIR clients. "It's just an initial version, ... Do you think this is something `<their people>` could use?" Never close by asking what they make of a feature; that asks them to review the product, which is a bigger request than it looks.
- Never close on the build story, on why you made it, or on a reaction-to-the-product question.

## 3. Learned corrections (append-only, dated)

- 2026-08-20: The connection note closes on the ASK, not on the build story. He cut the whole "why I built it" clause. Mine: "I built your course as an app to see whether therapists would actually use it between cases. It already exists." -> His: "I made an initial version of the Running Repairs app, want me to send it over?"
- 2026-08-20: Name the app after THEIR method, never "a demo app". Generic: "I made a demo app around your method." His: "the Running Repairs app", "the Only You app", "the Airway Mouth Doctor app", "the Mini Residency app". The possessive does the personalization work that an extra sentence used to.
- 2026-08-20: Say "initial version", not "demo". A demo is a sales prop; an initial version is their product already underway. Rotate the phrasing across a batch: "I made an initial version of..." / "I built a first version of..." / "I put together an early version of..." / "There's a first version of ... sitting ready" / "I made a first cut of...".
- 2026-08-20: Batch-level tell, caught by Alfonso reading five notes side by side ("is that the best you can think of"). Four of five ended in the identical sentence "It already exists." Rotating the OPENER is not enough; the CLOSING construction must vary across the batch too, because prospects in the same niche know each other.
- 2026-08-26: The message that DELIVERS the link closes on a low-commitment yes about THEIR clients, not on a curiosity question about the product. Mine: "Here it is <link>, curious what you make of the nervous system side?" -> His: "It's just an initial version, I laid it out as your twelve weeks so each one carries between the sessions. Do you think this is something the women in Fabulous 9 could use?" Two moves in one line: down-rank the artifact ("just an initial version") so there is nothing to judge yet, then ask a question they can say yes to without committing to anything. Personalize the middle clause with THEIR own structure (her twelve weeks), so the ask is about their programme rather than our build.
- 2026-08-26: Given two closes, he took the one that does not put a claim in the prospect's mouth. Rejected: "I built it around the between-sessions gap, which is where you said founders and CEOs usually lose it. Do you think this is something your women could use?" Chosen: the plain version with no assertion about her clients. Do not make someone agree with your read of their own business in order to answer your question.
