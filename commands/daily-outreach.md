Run the Tribed daily outreach pipeline. Follow references/pipeline.md (Job C: PREFLIGHT → QUOTA FILL → REPLY BACKLOG → leg 1 SHIP → DRAFT → leg 2 → REPORT) exactly. **The run's first deliverable is feeding the rails, and the standing requirement is the full daily quota on every account, every day** ("The rails and the morning quota fill" in pipeline.md): the hosted drips — LinkedIn both sessions, Instagram sequences, X — only ship what this session prepares, and an unfed rail idles silently. **The LinkedIn work runs as TWO sequential legs, one per session.** `data.li_account` is the owner field: absent or `"digital_university"` is leg 1 (Alfonso's founder session, driven by the `linkedin` MCP), `"martin_guerrero"` is leg 2 (Martin's VA session, sent by the hosted drip). The predicates are complements, so no lead is ever worked twice. Filter EVERY list a leg builds by it, run the legs sequentially (never concurrently, they share one Chromium host), and read each session's health and ramp separately with `get_linkedin_session_health({ linkedinAccount })` — never one read for both. This is an unattended run: every first-touch message is written to the review queue on the Tribed MCP (`accountId "digital_university"`) before anything sends, so the exact copy is always on the record. LinkedIn connection notes (`li_dm`) may then be approved by the run itself with `approve_outreach_draft` under Alfonso's direct-send authorization (2026-08-06) and shipped in the same run, up to the server-enforced daily cap. IG DMs and email still ship only after a human approves them in the dashboard (admin.tribed.io, Messaging > Review). LinkedIn sends through our self-hosted `linkedin` MCP; the tracker is the sequencer.

Preflight, LinkedIn: one `get_my_profile` call. On a session/login error, stop and tell the user to re-auth (`uvx mcp-server-linkedin@latest --import-from-browser`).

Preflight, LinkedIn EGRESS (per session, and do this before you trust any ramp number): read `get_linkedin_session_health({ linkedinAccount })` and look at the `proxy` block, NOT at `ok` / `sessionError` / `proxyError`. Those three read clean straight through a dead rail: `proxyError` is the state at that instant and one interleaved success clears it, so a residential line that drops out in bursts shows null between bursts. On 2026-08-27 the founder session reported `ok: true`, `sessionError: null`, `proxyError: null` and a watchdog with 0 failures while every invite deferred on the proxy, and the day sent ZERO against a ramp of 15. The two fields that tell the truth are `proxy.failuresLastHour` (above 0 means the line has been refusing inside the window even if it answers right now) and `proxy.stale` (true means the engine has not completed a single call in an hour, i.e. it missed a whole drip window). Report a non-zero count or a stale rail BY NAME at the top of the run, before the quota block, and treat it as a rail to fix rather than a session to stand down — the leads are fine, the pipe is not. A missing `proxy` block is a worker older than 2026-08-27: report it as UNKNOWN, never as healthy.

Preflight, Instagram (TWO steps, and step two is the one that gets skipped): `get_instagram_session_account_health({ accountId })` per account in `configuredAccounts`. A MISSING `queueingBlocked` or `actionControls` field is a stale MCP build, so it BLOCKS that account and gets reported by name, never read as healthy. `queueingBlocked: true` with `queueingBlockedReason: "action_controls_suppressed"` is not a stand-down on its own: call `verify_instagram_action_controls({ accountId })` first and honour the hold only if `after.suppressed` is still true. That check is read-only on Instagram, never clicks, and does NOT restart the block's window. If it errors or the build lacks it, the hold stands. Red for any other reason (dead session, engagement disabled) stands the account down without verifying. On 2026-08-17 an unverified 72h hold built from three profiles, two already recovered, idled four days of IG sending against a free budget and a 45-lead pool. An IG hold never blocks the LinkedIn or email legs. Full gate in references/pipeline.md.

0. QUOTA FILL (right after preflight, before any leg sends — pipeline.md "The rails and the morning quota fill"). For EACH account: read the ramps and caps live (`get_linkedin_session_health` per session, including its `drip` block — `dueActionable`, `copyFreshToday`, `needsCopy` (the authoring backlog this run owns), `sentToday`, `lastSendAt`, `stalled` with its `stalledReason`, and the counted-apart `dueMissingUsername` / `dueWithReply`; a `drip.ok: false` is a failed tracker read, never a zero backlog; `get_instagram_session_account_health` per IG account; `get_x_session_account_health` for X). Then feed every rail:
   - **LinkedIn copy, both sessions:** author fresh same-day copy and stamp `data.li_copy_at` ISO-now in the same write for EVERY due drip-owned lead — `to_invite` notes AND `awaiting_accept` follow-up 1s AND `await_fu2` follow-up 2s. Follow-ups first; authoring only invites is the 2026-08-24 failure that left 22 due founder follow-ups parked and the founder session at zero. Authoring means re-reading the lead (reply check, anchor still true), then re-stamp or rewrite — never a blind bulk re-stamp, and never a looser freshness gate.
   - **LinkedIn invites:** stage enough new `to_invite` leads with fresh notes to fill each session's `limits.invitesPerDay`, per session, never summed.
   - **Instagram:** schedule enough sequences (`schedule_instagram_lead_sequence`, from approved `ig_dm` drafts) to spend each account's `caps` `remainingToday`, behind the two-step gate.
   - **X:** keep a due queue of channel `"x"` leads staged on `digital_university` (`data.x_username`, `data.x_comment` written about the person, not one post) so the x-drip never idles empty.
1. SHIP (order matters — replies first):
   - **The reply backlog (unibox, BOTH channels) — this runs before anything else in SHIP.** Full spec in pipeline.md "1.0 The reply backlog". `unibox_sync_instagram({ accountId, preserveUnread: true })` first (an unsynced IG inbox proves nothing; a sync carrying `error` means UNKNOWN, never empty), then `unibox_list_threads({ communityId: "digital_university", state: "open", sort: "oldestWaiting", limit: 100 })`, paging `nextCursor` while `truncated` — that sort FILTERS to `awaitingReply=true`, so it IS the reply queue. Autoresponder and away-bot threads get `unibox_set_state({ state: "done", markRead: true })` and no reply. **`awaitingReply` is NOT proof nobody answered** - before drafting, open each surviving thread's lead (`get_outreach_lead` on `leadRef`, else `list_outreach_leads({ search })`) and read its TOUCH LOG and `nextActionAt`; a logged reply or a future next-action means the thread is handled and the unibox is just behind, so drop it. On 2026-08-24, 11 of 13 apparent human threads were already answered or deliberately deferred, and one had soft-declined the day before. Every real thread that survives that gate gets `unibox_get_thread` read IN FULL (the listing snippet truncates the actual ask) and one reply draft via `queue_outreach_drafts` — channel `kind`, the SAME `externalId` the cold drafts use, `slot: "reply"`, `language` matching the language they wrote in — left `pending` for the dashboard, never auto-approved (`approve_outreach_draft` takes `li_dm` connection notes only). Answer the question that was asked; a nudge back at a concrete ask about pricing or scope burns the warmest lead in the pipeline. Then stop that lead's sequence (`log_outreach_touch`, `advanceTo: "replied"`, `automated: false`, `data.li_state: "human"`) so no cold follow-up lands mid-conversation, and leave the thread `open` — a queued draft is not a sent reply. Cap 25/run, oldest first, carry on `reply_backlog_cursor`. **The LinkedIn passes below cannot substitute for this**: they build their watch list from the tracker and are LinkedIn-only, so an Instagram thread or an inbound from someone with no lead record is unreachable by construction. That is how 19 threads reached 32 days waiting with 34 pending drafts and not one reply among them (2026-08-24) - but read pipeline.md's "Why this step exists" before trusting that count, because most of those threads had in fact been answered and the unibox had not caught up.
   - Replies — three passes, because neither messaging tool paginates (`get_inbox` returns the ~10-20 newest and never clicks "Load more conversations"; `search_conversations` returns 10 rows whatever `limit` says). **A** `get_inbox({ limit: 50 })` for today's replies and anyone not on the tracker. **B (the real sweep)** every li lead at stage `replied` / `interested` / `demo_request`, plus any lead with a `data.demo_url` never sent: `get_conversation({ thread_id })` when `data.li_thread_id` is stored, else walk the lookup ladder — stored id > the `get_inbox` rows Pass A already returned > a CONTENT search on a distinctive phrase from that lead's own stored `data.li_followup_1` / `data.li_note` > a name `search_conversations({ keywords: "<name>", limit: 2 })` — taking the id off `references` (the `sections` text lies, it reads "No messages…yet!" on hits), storing it, then reading the thread. Run them cheapest-first and stop at the first HIT. **A hit at any rung is conclusive; an empty at any rung is worth nothing.** A name search reaches OLD threads the inbox window cannot, but it also misses live ones intermittently (2026-08-21: "Nikki Hoxie" and "Hoxie" both returned LinkedIn's empty state while her thread sat in `get_inbox`, and a content search on `"Rebuild Strong"` found it). **HARD RULE: never clear a `*_sent_at` stamp, write a "never delivered" / phantom-send note, or reset `li_state` backwards on a name-search miss** — the content search on the lead's stored copy must come back empty too, and the note must record which lookups ran. Sara Freed lost a true `li_followup_1_sent_at` this way on 2026-08-21; the send was real and a content search on `"another marriage coach"` found the thread. Cap 40/run, carry the rest on `reply_sweep_cursor`. **C** one rotated `search_conversations({ keywords: "demo" | "app" | "built" | "tribed", limit: 10 })` sampler. Then advance stage + store `data.li_thread_id`, draft Mode 2 replies for the user (never auto-send). Interested + no demo → build it (Job D, max 5/run). Demo built but never sent = a reply already missed; draft the delivery.
   - Acceptances: due leads awaiting acceptance → READ the connection degree (`get_person_profile`, or `search_people` with `network: ["F"]`). Only "· 1st" is an acceptance; a delivered message, a working or unavailable composer, and a reply prove nothing. If 1st: write `data.li_degree: "1st"` + `li_degree_checked_at` + `li_degree_source: "profile_top_card"` + `li_accepted_at` in one call, then `send_message` `data.li_followup_1`. **Read the payload `status` before writing anything: only `"sent"` earns a `*_sent_at` stamp or a stage advance.** `send_failed`, `recipient_resolution_failed` and `recipient_urn_mismatch` are not sends — no stamp, retry next run. `send_unconfirmed` means the submit was issued and then the browser threw, so the message MAY be with them: stamp nothing, park the lead (`automated: false` AND `data.li_state: "human"`), verify from the thread, never resend blind — a duplicate DM cannot be taken back. `recipient_selected` is set when a row is clicked in the compose PICKER, before the text is typed; it is never evidence of delivery and `send_unconfirmed` carries it as `true`. After a `"sent"`, re-read the thread (`get_conversation` by `data.li_thread_id`, else the ladder) and put both in the touch note, quoting the status verbatim and naming the field that proved each half (`sent — status: "sent"; thread re-read clean via get_conversation on data.li_thread_id …`), mirroring the drip's post-send `checkThread`. Not visible yet is NOT a phantom (a real send can take ~20 min): note it, leave the stamp, recheck next run. Then schedule follow-up 2 (+3-4d), same rule on that send. Reads 2nd or anything else: record that degree and clear any `li_accepted_at` in the same write, bump a day. Badge unreadable: stamp nothing, bump a day. 21+ days pending → lost.
   - Due follow-up 2: only for leads whose stored `data.li_degree` is `"1st"` — anything else gets its degree re-read instead of the closer. Then verify no reply via `get_conversation`, send `data.li_followup_2`, archive-if-quiet in 14d.
   - Approved drafts (`list_outreach_drafts({ status: "approved" })` + "error" retries): li_dm → `connect_with_person` with the note + upsert lead with follow-ups in `data`; ig_dm → upsert to the VA's list (owner from shipTarget); email → push to the Instantly campaign. `mark_outreach_draft_shipped` per draft — and when the attempt PROVED nothing was sent (`note_sent: false` with `already_connected`, top card "· 1st", invite already pending), report it `ok: false` with `provenNoSend: true` + an `errorKind`, which hands the direct-send slot back to today's budget; never pass it for a failure that might have sent (ambiguous, timeout, unknown), because a refunded phantom send pushes the run past LinkedIn's real limits. A draft that can never ship (pending invite already out, profile gone, replied elsewhere) → `reject_outreach_draft` with a concrete `reason` (add `provenNoSend: true` when nothing was ever sent); never reject an `approved` draft.
   Caps: 15 connection requests, 25 messages, 25 IG handoffs, 50 emails per run — but the real budget is `limits.invitesPerDay` / `limits.messagesPerDay` off THAT session's `get_linkedin_session_health({ linkedinAccount })`, and **ramps are per account, never shared or summed** (2026-08-21: founder 9 days old at 5/day, martin_guerrero 1 day old at 3/day, so a 5 and a 3, never an 8). Counters are UTC-day keyed PER SESSION: an early-morning local run lands in the previous UTC day, so a second run the same local day can double that session's ramp. One run per UTC day per session — check `sessions["<account>"].utcDay` in the parsed `daily_run` state, and do not trust `approve_outreach_draft`'s `remainingToday` for this, it counts pool approvals and is blind to the split. A `connect_unavailable` on a creator profile almost never means closed to requests — it means Follow is the primary button and Connect sits under the More menu; read the payload's `observed_state` / `connect_route` before treating the lead as uninvitable, and never reject a draft on `connect_unavailable` alone. A sent message can take ~20 minutes to appear in the thread — "not in thread" minutes after `sent: true` is NOT a phantom send; never resend on a fresh thread read, recheck next run. STOP the LinkedIn leg on any checkpoint/captcha/verification error and report it.

2. DRAFT — IG-first cross-channel discovery plus rotated `search_people` sourcing (rotation via `get_outreach_state("query_rotation")`). Skip-check each candidate (`list_outreach_leads({ search })`), ICP-gate (2,000+ followers OR creator/influencer flag; Apify `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-scraper` for bulk), personalize survivors in the Tribed voice (rule zero: one real anchor; connection note + case-study follow-up + video follow-up), and queue with `queue_outreach_drafts` (kinds li_dm / ig_dm / email). Anchor-less prospects queue with anchor omitted (held) — never write generic.

   Discovery runs ONCE for the run, then ownership is DECIDED (leg 2 writes it): Martin's share of today's new leads is `limits.invitesPerDay` off `get_linkedin_session_health({ linkedinAccount: "martin_guerrero" })`, the founder's share is the founder's own number, and the two are planned separately and never added. Give Martin the plainest, least founder-voiced notes; anything anchored on Alfonso's own content stays on the founder session, because the note has to be true from the mouth of whoever sends it. Come out of DRAFT with a named list of Martin's drafts and nothing else — no approval, no upsert, no stamp — so exactly one place in the run can set `data.li_account`. Founder leads are left unstamped; do not bulk re-stamp them with `"digital_university"`.

   Then ship the FOUNDER session's first touches, within whatever invite budget phase 1 left of the founder ramp: `approve_outreach_draft({ draftIds: [<the founder-owned li_dm drafts just queued>], accountId: "digital_university" })`, read `remainingToday` from the result (it is the pool's approval cap, NOT a session budget — `limits.invitesPerDay` is the ceiling on what actually goes out), then ship each approved one exactly as in phase 1's li_dm step. Anything refused comes back with its reason — act on it, never route around it. Every proven-no-send you report while shipping puts its slot back, so if drafts were refused with `daily_cap_reached`, re-run `approve_outreach_draft` on those ids after shipping and send whatever it now allows, inside the worker's ramp. `ig_dm` and `email` drafts are NOT approved here; they stay pending for the dashboard.

2b. LEG 2 (`martin_guerrero`) — runs only AFTER leg 1 has finished and `close_session` has been called, never concurrently (one Chromium host). Skip it if preflight stood that session down: a missing `get_linkedin_session_health` tool means a stale MCP build, and `No LinkedIn worker is configured for account "martin_guerrero"` means `LINKEDIN_WORKERS` is unset on the deployed MCP. Report either by name and NEVER fall back to the founder session. This leg drives no browser — `get_linkedin_session_health` is the only MCP surface taking a `linkedinAccount`, and the `linkedin` MCP is hardwired to the founder session, so Martin's sends are performed by the hosted drip. Read his health and ramp with the account named, check `sessions["martin_guerrero"].utcDay` in the parsed `daily_run` state, then work ONLY leads where `data.li_account === "martin_guerrero"`: read their acceptances and replies out of the tracker (`li_degree`, `li_state`, the drip's touch log, leads it parked `human`) rather than probing LinkedIn, and route every Martin reply to the human queue. Then write the assignment DRAFT decided: `approve_outreach_draft`, upsert with `data.li_account: "martin_guerrero"` + `li_state: "to_invite"` + `li_copy_at` stamped today (the drip refuses copy older than the current UTC day), then `mark_outreach_draft_shipped({ ok: true, detail: "queued to martin_guerrero drip" })` — that is STAGED, not sent, so never report it as an invite sent this run. Never degree-read or thread-read a Martin lead from the founder browser; degree and threads are relative to the viewing account, so that is a wrong answer, not a partial one.

3. REPORT — `set_outreach_state({ key: "daily_run", ... })` with per-channel counts plus a `sessions` block (`{ "<account>": { at, utcDay, invitesSent | invitesStaged, rampInvites, standDown? } }`) that the next run's per-session UTC-day check reads — stamp every session a leg ran for, stand-downs included (note: `get_outreach_state` returns `value` as a JSON STRING — parse it, and trust `updatedAt` for when it was written), snapshot the funnel with `get_outreach_lead_counts` → `upsert_outreach_metrics` (never tally `list_outreach_leads` pages — that reports a floor as a total), and `send_admin_report`: the quota utilization block FIRST — every rail on every account as cap vs authored/staged vs sent, `drip.stalled` sessions flagged by name, every below-cap rail carrying its missing ingredient, drip-owned sends reported as staged rather than sent — then one block per sending session (health, `warmup.sessionAgeDays`, that session's `limits.invitesPerDay`/`limits.messagesPerDay`, invites SENT for the founder and invites STAGED for Martin, plus Martin's drip-parked `human` leads) written as two separate numbers and never a sum, then the reply-backlog line (threads still awaiting a reply, split by platform, oldest wait in days, count over 24h, reply drafts queued, autoresponder threads closed by name, threads past their Meta `windowExpiresAt` needing a manual send, and any `reply_backlog_cursor` carry — and when the oldest wait is over 7 days that number LEADS the digest, above the quota block), then replies (split by pass), the acceptance line with its denominator (checked / accepted / unreadable / still unswept — never a bare "zero acceptances"), sends, invites, drafts queued/held (nudge to the Review tab), drafts rejected with reasons, demos built, stale invites, errors. Then `close_session` — the LinkedIn browser session is never left alive after the run.

Never infer a LinkedIn acceptance from messaging success. Only a read connection degree of "· 1st" counts, and the proof (`li_degree`, `li_degree_checked_at`, `li_degree_source`) goes on the lead beside every `li_accepted_at` — and it is on YOU to write it: the MCP accepts an acceptance stamp with no proof attached and no complaint, so nothing but this discipline stops a false stamp. Do not work out-of-ICP prospects. The run may approve its own `li_dm` drafts and nothing else — `approve_outreach_draft` refuses every other kind, and it can never un-approve, rewrite, or resurrect a draft a human decided on.
